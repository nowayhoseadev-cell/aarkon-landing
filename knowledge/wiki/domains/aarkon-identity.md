# AARKON Identity

AARKON is positioned as an independent software lab, not a consulting agency, portfolio, or hype-oriented startup.

## Canonical Positioning

AARKON is an independent software lab building local-first tools, verification infrastructure, and operational systems.

## Tone

The public identity should remain minimal, technical, restrained, and broadly extensible. Copy should favor clarity, trust, and careful engineering over narrow category claims or startup theater.
